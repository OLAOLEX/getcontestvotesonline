Privacy.

Updated 15 November 2026.

Your inputs

The current site processes the quote form and planner in your browser. The homepage starter keeps only your contest details in this browser tab’s session storage for up to 10 minutes to carry them to the quote page. They are consumed on arrival; contact details are not saved. If browser storage is unavailable, continue on the quote page and enter the contest again.

Contacting us

Reviewing a request does not send it. Choosing Send via WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with the contest and contact details shown in your review, including your name and reply email, in its message link. WhatsApp receives that link when you open it. Send the message there to contact our team. Its processing is governed by WhatsApp’s privacy policy. Share only the contest information needed for your request.

Enquiries

We use information you choose to send to respond and manage agreed services. Contact us through the same WhatsApp conversation for questions about your enquiry information.

Analytics and other forms

No analytics or advertising scripts are installed in this preview. When you choose Send quote request, your name, reply email, contest details, optional contact information and request reference are sent to Formspree to process the enquiry for our team. Formspree acceptance confirms receipt by that provider, not email inbox delivery. See Formspree’s privacy policy. Do not send credentials, payment-card data or unrelated private information.
