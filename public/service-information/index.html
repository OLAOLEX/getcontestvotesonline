Service information.

Quotes and orders

An enquiry is not an order or payment. Agree the entry, work, quantity, final price and currency, timing, update channel and terms in writing before proceeding.

Free trial: up to 100 votes

The trial amount depends on voting type, contest eligibility, access requirements and available time. Some contests or vote types may not qualify. We confirm the amount and applicable conditions before starting. A paid continuation requires a separate agreement.

Delivery and changes

Availability and the delivery window are confirmed for each campaign. Inform us when the voting page, category, round or deadline changes. No universal start time or winning outcome is promised.

Refund arrangements

If agreed work cannot be delivered, the refund terms in your written quote explain the applicable arrangement. Confirm these before payment; this page does not create an unconditional full-refund guarantee.

Participation and access

Campaigns must follow the organiser’s requirements. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, private account access or payment-card details. Political, government and regulated elections are outside this service.
