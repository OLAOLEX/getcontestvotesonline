Daily Contest Voting: When to Send Your Next Reminder

Understand daily voting limits, reset times and simple supporter reminders before asking people to vote again.

Does daily voting mean every morning?

Not always. Some contests reset at midnight in the organiser’s time zone. Others allow another vote only after a set number of hours. Before sending daily reminders, read the rules and any message shown after voting. If the reset time is unclear, ask the organiser.

For example, a rolling 24-hour limit means someone who voted at 8 pm may not be able to vote at 9 am the next morning. A calendar-day reset can work differently. Do not assume that your local midnight is the contest’s reset time.

Keep a small campaign note

Record the official link, entry name, category, round, closing time and repeat-voting rule in one place. Update it if the organiser changes the deadline. You can then use the same accurate details in every reminder instead of copying an outdated post.

Ask supporters whether they want reminders

A willing supporter may appreciate a daily message; somebody else may only want the first link. Ask before adding people to a reminder list. Choose a sensible time, keep the message short and stop when voting ends or someone asks you to stop.

Today’s voting is open for [contest]. If you’d like to vote again for [entry], here’s the link: [link]. The rules allow [repeat limit], and the round closes [deadline]. Thank you!

Check the right round and category

A contest can keep the same website address when it moves into a final round. Open the ballot before your next reminder and confirm that your entry is still listed. If an old link goes to the previous round, replace it. Tell supporters about any new registration or confirmation step.

Plan around the time left

Divide your target across the remaining days to make the workload easier to understand. That calculation is a plan, not a prediction: other entries can keep gaining votes too. Use the free vote planner with your own quantity, or read what to do when voting closes today for an urgent enquiry.

If you request paid support, include the daily limit and exact closing time in your contest quote request. The proposed participation must fit the organiser’s rules.
