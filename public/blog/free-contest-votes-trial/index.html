Up to 100 contest votes free: what is checked first?

Understand trial eligibility, the agreed amount and the next step before a paid campaign.

Request a trial for your actual entry

Send the contest link or native-poll description and identify the entry or option. Our trial can provide up to 100 contest votes free, with the available amount depending on vote type, contest requirements, access and time remaining.

“Up to 100” is a maximum, not a promise that every request receives 100 votes. Some competitions or voting actions may not qualify. We confirm the available trial amount and terms before starting.

What a trial can help you discuss

Use the conversation to clarify the proposed voting action, what updates can be supplied and the scope of a possible paid campaign. A small trial cannot establish a final contest outcome or guarantee that every ballot will survive the organiser’s review.

Keep a paid order separate

Trying an eligible service does not automatically start a paid campaign. If you want to continue, agree the quantity, full price, delivery schedule and applicable refund terms in writing.

Use the trial enquiry option to start the conversation. Never send login credentials, confirmation codes or payment-card details in a contest brief.
