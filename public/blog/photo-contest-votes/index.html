Photo Contest Votes: Get Your Entry Ready for More Support

Prepare a clear photo-contest voting link, explain the steps to supporters and send the right details when asking for a quote.

Make it easy to find your photo

A photo contest may have hundreds of entries. Sending people to the gallery homepage gives them another job before they can help. Open your photo, look for its share link and test that link in a private browser window. Check that the picture and entry name are visible to someone who is not signed in.

If there is no separate entry page, tell supporters the exact name, category and way to find it. Do not rely on “the third photo” because the gallery order may change.

Show the entry in your request

Use your own photo or an image you have permission to share. Explain the contest in one or two sentences and put the voting link nearby. Tell people about registration or email confirmation before they start, so they know what to expect.

My photo [entry name] is in [contest]. If you’d like to support it, you can vote here: [entry link]. Voting closes [date, time and time zone]. Thank you for taking a look.

For more wording options, see our messages for asking for votes.

Check what public voting decides

Read whether votes choose the winner, select a shortlist or contribute to a judged result. Those are different situations. Also check whether the rules permit repeat votes and outside promotion. A campaign target should match the current stage of the competition.

Woobox’s photo/video contest setup guide shows that organisers can configure gallery visibility and voting restrictions. Your contest’s own instructions are the place to confirm what applies to your entry.

Considering buying photo contest votes?

Where outside support is permitted, send the direct photo link, entry name, requested quantity and deadline. Include any voter-country or confirmation requirements. Ask for a total price and delivery schedule before paying, and agree how updates will be shared.

At Get Contest Votes Online, you can request a quote for your photo entry or ask about an eligible trial. If voting closes soon, include the exact time in your first message. A vote order does not guarantee a winning position.
