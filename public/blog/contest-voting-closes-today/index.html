Voting closes today: what should your enquiry include?

Give the exact closing time first so availability can be reviewed quickly.

Put the deadline at the beginning

Write the complete date, time and organiser’s time zone. “Tonight” means different things to people in different countries. Also distinguish the end of the current voting round from the overall competition’s closing date.

Keep the request short and specific

An effective urgent enquiry might say: “Please review [official entry link], [entry name], [category]. Voting closes [date] at [time and time zone]. I’m considering [quantity], or please advise what scope is available.” Replace the bracketed details before sending.

Include any known registration, email confirmation or voter-location requirement. If your quantity refers to separate participants rather than repeat ballots, make that clear.

Confirm availability before paying

An urgent request is not a confirmed delivery slot. We review the voting action, quantity and available time before agreeing the work. Keep the quoted start, completion window and update arrangements together.

If an organiser changes the round or closes voting early, tell us in the same conversation. Do not interpret a planning calculation as a delivery commitment or a winning prediction.
