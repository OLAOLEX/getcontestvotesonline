How to Get Votes for an Online Contest

Get more contest votes with a clear entry link, supporter messages, reminders and a plan for the time you have left.

Start with a link people can use

Open your entry on the official contest website. Check the name, category and voting round, then copy the direct link. Test it in a private browser window so you know what a new visitor sees. Tell supporters if they need to register or confirm their email.

Ask people who already know you

Start with friends, customers, colleagues or followers who may want to support your entry. Explain what you entered and why it matters to you. One clear request is easier to act on than a long message with several links.

A simple message you can adapt

I’ve entered [contest name] with [entry name]. If you’d like to support me, you can vote here: [direct link]. Voting closes [date, time and time zone]. Thank you for taking a look.

Replace the brackets before sending. If the contest requires email confirmation, add one short sentence explaining that step. Share in groups only where promotional posts are welcome.

Make your posts easy to follow

Use a picture of your actual entry and place the voting link where readers can find it. Keep the entry name consistent across posts. A short pinned post can answer the same questions without making supporters search through older updates.

Plan reminders around the voting rules

Check whether people can vote once, once per calendar day or after a rolling time limit. Remind willing supporters only when another vote is allowed. Include the closing time in your final reminder and stop asking once the round has ended.

Work out a realistic target

If vote totals are public, record your count and the leader’s count at the same time. Recheck later to understand how quickly the gap is changing. Hidden totals and judging rounds make predictions less reliable. A target helps you plan; it cannot guarantee a win.

Considering paid contest voting support?

Read the organiser’s rules before ordering outside support. Where it is permitted, send the entry link, quantity, deadline and any country or registration requirements. Ask for the full price, delivery schedule and updates before paying. Do not share your account password.
