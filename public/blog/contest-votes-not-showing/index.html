Contest Votes Not Showing? Check These Steps First

If a contest vote count has not changed, check the entry, confirmation steps, voting limit and result display before trying again.

Start with the message after voting

When you press vote and the number stays the same, it is tempting to keep pressing. First, read the page. It may say that your vote was received, that another step is needed or that you have already voted. Those messages tell you more than an unchanged counter on its own.

Make sure you are looking at the right entry

Open the direct link and check the name, category and round. A business can appear in several award categories, and a photo contest can have separate qualifying and final rounds. A vote on one page may not change the number on another.

Save the correct entry link with your campaign notes. If you need help identifying it, see which contest link to use.

Check email confirmation and daily limits

If the voting page asks for email confirmation, complete the official step yourself. Check your spam folder if the message has not arrived. Do not forward private confirmation links or codes to somebody offering to fix the vote.

A repeat vote may also be too early. Some contests allow one vote overall; others use daily or rolling limits. Read the rule rather than assuming that midnight in your country resets it. Our daily voting guide explains what to record for reminders.

A public total is not always a live receipt

The organiser may hide totals or update the display separately. Check the contest’s help page for an explanation. An unchanged number does not by itself prove that a vote failed, and a higher number does not identify who cast each vote.

Give the organiser something specific to check

Send the entry URL, the date and time of your attempt, and the exact message you saw. Mention your browser and device if there was a loading error. Redact email addresses and other private details from screenshots. Ask whether the vote was accepted and whether the counter is expected to update immediately.

If you have an order with our team, keep your enquiry reference and reply in the same conversation. We can review the agreed work and updates with you; only the organiser controls its accepted vote records and final results.
