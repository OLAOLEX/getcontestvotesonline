How to Ask for Votes: Messages You Can Make Your Own

Simple contest-vote requests for friends, customers and social followers, with examples and a final reminder.

Tell people what you entered

A good request sounds like something you would actually say. Name the contest, explain your entry and give people a direct voting link. You do not need a dramatic story or a long sales pitch. A short personal reason is enough.

Before sharing, open the link yourself and check the voting steps. If supporters need to confirm an email or select a category, mention that next to the link. See our guide to finding the right contest link if the ballot has several rounds.

A message for friends

I’ve entered [contest] with [entry name]. If you’d like to support me, here’s the voting link: [link]. It closes on [date and time]. Thank you!

Add a sentence about your entry if it feels right. Send it to people who know you and would welcome the request. Give them room to choose whether to vote.

A request for customers

We’re nominated for [award] in the [category] category. If you’ve enjoyed visiting us, we’d appreciate your support. You can find [business name] here: [link]. Voting ends [deadline]. Thanks for being part of our community.

Use the exact business name shown on the ballot. If you are nominated in several categories, choose the most relevant one for that message rather than making customers search through the whole awards list.

A social media caption

My entry for [contest] is now open for voting. You can see it and vote here: [link]. Voting closes [deadline]. Thanks to everyone who has already supported it.

Pair the caption with your own entry picture. Follow the group or page’s posting rules, and avoid repeatedly tagging people who have not asked for reminders.

A final reminder

A quick reminder: voting for [contest] closes at [time and time zone] today. If you still want to support [entry], here’s the link: [link]. Thank you for all the support.

Only ask people to vote again when the rules allow another vote. For repeated rounds, read our daily voting reminder guide. If you need help planning the wider campaign, start with how to get votes for an online contest.
