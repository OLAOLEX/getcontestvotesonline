Before you order

A few useful answers to get you started.

How much do contest votes cost?

The price depends on the quantity, voting steps and deadline. Send your contest link for a quote.

Can I ask for a custom quantity?

Yes. Tell us how many votes you want, or leave the quantity open and ask us for advice.

Can I get votes before my deadline?

Send the exact closing date, time and time zone. We’ll check availability and confirm delivery timing before you order.

How does the free trial work?

Ask for up to 100 contest votes free. We’ll check your contest and confirm the available amount and terms first.

Do you guarantee a win?

No. We agree the work and delivery details with you. The organiser decides which votes count and the final result.
