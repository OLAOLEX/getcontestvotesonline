QUESTIONS ABOUT BUYING CONTEST VOTES

Before you get started.

How can I buy contest votes online?

Send your entry and deadline for review. We confirm available support, total price and timing before you agree an order.

Can I try up to 100 contest votes free?

Yes, ask about a free trial. The available amount depends on vote type, participation conditions, access and time remaining. Some requests do not qualify; we confirm the amount and terms first.

How much will my contest cost?

The quantity, voting action and schedule affect the quote. Ask for a total for your entry; no generic price or calculator output replaces the written quote.

Do I need to know the quantity?

No. Leave the quantity blank and ask us to review the request. The optional planner can calculate a daily target and budget from your own inputs.

Do you work with countries outside the USA?

We offer worldwide contest support. Tell us any country, local participation or account requirement so the scope matches the actual ballot.

Do you guarantee a win?

No. The organiser controls eligibility, counting, judging and final results. We agree the campaign work and reporting, not a winning position.
