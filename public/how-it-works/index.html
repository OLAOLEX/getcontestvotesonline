HOW IT WORKS

From your link
to a clear plan.

1. Send the entry

Use the short quote form or contact us directly on WhatsApp. Include your quantity if known and the closing date, time and time zone. A native poll can be described when it has no public link.

2. Confirm the available scope

We review the action and requirements. Your written quote identifies the entry, quantity or scope, total price, schedule, update arrangements and applicable refund terms.

3. Agree before ordering

Ask questions and confirm the details before paying. A trial request is reviewed separately and does not automatically begin a paid campaign.

4. Follow campaign updates

Continue in the same conversation. Tell us about any change to the ballot or deadline. Screenshots show agreed activity where available; the organiser determines final accepted results.
