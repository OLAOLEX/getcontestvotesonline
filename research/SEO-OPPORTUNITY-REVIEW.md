# Get Contest Votes Online: SEO opportunity review

Reviewed 42 candidates across parallel commercial, platform and audience lanes. These are research briefs, not 42 approved articles. Homepage and public site files were not edited.

Demand is qualitative. No search volumes, ranking guarantees or traffic estimates have been invented. Sister-site coverage is a differentiation constraint, not an instruction to copy its text.

## Publication rules

- Merge daily-reset, urgent-deadline, pricing and registration repetitions into their existing owners unless the reader task is materially different.
- Woobox missing-entry and approval-stage ideas should be one article, not two.
- Do not create generic versions of sister-site pet, photo or Readers’ Choice guides; use a distinct scenario and original examples.
- Hold noncanonical BBC mirror material; fetch canonical primary evidence first.
- Country examples, contest dates and platform settings are examples, never universal service promises.
- Do not use organizer restrictions as instructions for bypassing them.
- Keep homepage untouched; link new articles from the blog index, relevant existing articles and sitemap.
- Put quote CTAs only where a service enquiry fits. Direct technical disputes to the organiser.
